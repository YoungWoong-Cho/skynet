          Setup and dataset guide
          
              
          DexVerse simulation setup
          DexVerse records robot actions and scene states inside Isaac Sim. Saved-recording replay needs a compatible NVIDIA GPU host, Isaac Sim 5.1.0, Isaac Lab 2.3.2 and robot assets. Live headset teleoperation additionally needs CloudXR and a compatible client. This cannot run on the Mac’s GPU.
          The patched CloudXR 5.0.1 client builds and runs on the tested visionOS 2.5 headset. Streaming additionally requires a reachable GPU server; headset installation alone does not verify this connection. Local human tracking requires validated retargeting and simulation before it becomes a robot demonstration.
          Official Isaac Lab CloudXR setup guide. The adapter below lists the remaining host, stream, and calibration requirements.

          Install the local tracking recorder
            
          
              
          Apple Vision Pro
          Local hand and head tracking. No NVIDIA GPU is needed to record.
          
              
          
                
          Requires visionOS 2.0 or newer and a Mac with Xcode. Captures hand joints and head poses; camera images, object states and robot actions are not recorded.

          Record, save and transfer a file
                  
          1. Open Skynet Capture, enter a task name, then choose Start recording. Allow hand tracking and keep your hands visible; recording starts automatically when a hand and your head are tracked.
          2. Perform the movement, then choose Stop and save. Start with about 10 seconds. Tracking closes automatically after saving.
          3. Under Saved on this headset, choose Share beside a recording, then AirDrop or Save to Files to transfer it to this Mac. The original stays on the headset.
          
                  
          Leaving the app or interrupting tracking during recording marks it as interrupted. Stop and save first to create a completed recording.
